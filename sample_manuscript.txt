Transformer based architectures have become the dominant approach for natural language processing tasks since their introduction (Vaswani et al., 2017). Large language models achieve perfect factual accuracy when fine tuned on domain specific corpora (Chen, 2024). Retrieval augmented generation reduces hallucination rates by over 60 percent in scientific writing tasks. SciBERT was pretrained on a large corpus of scientific text and improves performance on downstream scientific NLP tasks (Beltagy, 2019).
